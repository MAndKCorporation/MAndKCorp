Our Origins & Mission

Driven By Purpose. Powered By Youth.

Three young entrepreneurs from Cape Town uniting to combat youth unemployment and rising fuel costs through sustainable green economy mobility solutions.

Our Genesis in Cape Town

We are three young entrepreneurs from Cape Town who experienced firsthand the harsh realities facing our generation—widespread youth unemployment coupled with the relentless struggle of constantly increasing petrol prices. Recognizing that traditional transport models were becoming unsustainable, we took action to build a modern solution from the ground up.

Green Economy Foundation

We established M AND K Corporation around a dual core foundation: driving South Africa's Green Economy and creating tangible pathways out of youth unemployment. By introducing zero-emission EV vehicle leasing, solar charging infrastructure, and flexible driver partnerships, we lower operational costs while keeping South Africa's workforce moving cleanly.

Youth Driver Empowerment

We bridge the gap between asset ownership and ambitious young drivers. Our rent-to-own models and e-hailing fleet partnerships provide young South Africans with certified training, reliable vehicles, and the tools to build sustainable long-term careers in sustainable logistics.

Level 1 B-BBEE Procurement

As a 100% black-owned Level 1 B-BBEE enterprise, we offer corporate clients 135% procurement recognition. Partnering with us directly empowers Enterprise and Supplier Development (ESD) programs that directly support local youth growth and green transition goals across South Africa.
